### Iteration 8 — Ocharo setup verb

For the Events integration (wow-two-ws `system/sessions/events-integration`, #20, #50). `ocharo setup --group <id>
[--schedules] [--run]` is a host verb on the image: it runs the same idempotent setup as `POST api/ocharo/sources/setup`
on the target's own settings, so a platform job provisions Ocharo's tenant and subscriptions without an operator
token. Its last stdout line carries the tenant, container, subscription and run ids. `product.md` § Use states the
internal-use rule: captures serve Ocharo's own tracking and review, and nothing sourced is republished.

Verification on 2026-11-10 against public SCFD `ffccfa6`: 13 source-host E2E tests pass, including the verb's
provisioning, repeat and usage cases.

On dev (`v0.1.4`, 2026-11-10) the verb provisioned tenant `ocharo` with the fashion and exhibitions-ru
subscriptions, and `--run` queued one capture each. Both runs ended at the 5-page budget with no failure, capturing
4 pages each. The fashion subscription holds one event ready for review; exhibitions-ru holds none, since its one
event was a past art show the filter drops. The next pin, `6254896`, writes the event's enums as strings.
