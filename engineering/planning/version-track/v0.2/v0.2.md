### Iteration 30 — Spend guard

- [x] Ability to cap the AI cost of one host with `Harvest:Processing:Budget:LimitUsd`, so one credit pays `post-tags`, `extract-event`, `extract-listing`, `rental-terms@2` and eval runs without being overrun
  - the guard sums the AI module's inference log over a monthly window that starts on `CycleStartDay` (default 1), or over everything since `SinceUtc`; it adds no table, and without a limit, or without shared AI, it is `off` and changes nothing
  - a reading is reused for `RefreshInterval` (1 minute), so the workers' polling never sums the log per claim
- [x] Ability to slow down at the soft threshold (`SoftShare`, default 0.8): a `post-tags` or extraction call tries only the last configured route
- [x] Ability to stop at the limit: `post-tags` and extraction claim nothing and leave their rows queued, `post-tags run`, `extract run` and the `rental-terms@2` stage run refuse (`Conflict`, or `budget-exhausted` for the stage) and call no model
  - a read of the log that fails keeps the last reading, or fails closed at `hard` with none
- [x] Ability to read the spend with `processing budget` (one JSON line, exit 0 for every state) and `GET api/processing/budget` (operator only), and to see the reading in a `processing rerun --dry-run`
  - the reading holds the state, the window, the limit, the soft threshold, the spend, the monthly projection and the cost per use case and model, in micro-USD and USD
- [x] Ability to reject a bad budget setting at `config check` (`LimitUsd`, `SoftShare`, `CycleStartDay`, `RefreshInterval`)
- [ ] Ability to read the spend guard on harvest-dev (`processing budget` with LimitUsd set)
